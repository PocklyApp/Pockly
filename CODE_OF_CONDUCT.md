# Code Of Conduct

Pockly follows the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1.

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while still asking for clear technical evidence.
- Keep discussions focused on the work.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of project discussion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior harms the
project or its contributors. Report conduct concerns through the maintainer
contacts listed on the project profile.
